YAMLResume is designed to be a complete, opinionated resume solution. To help
you understand how it fits into the broader landscape of resume tools and
formats, we provide detailed comparisons with existing alternatives.

Each comparison covers feature differences, design philosophy, AI workflows,
rendering engines, internationalization, developer APIs, and practical
trade-offs so you can choose the right tool for your workflow.
